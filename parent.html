ℹ️ How this works

Each session emits one code. Codes are signed so Bertie can't forge or replay them. The verifier checks the signature, decodes the stats, and applies your treat thresholds (above).

  • SM-... Small — solid session.
  • BIG-... Big — strong session that hit higher bars.
  • WIN-... Streak milestone — earned after 7 consecutive days of small-or-better.
  • BOSS-... Boss battle — 10-question mock test passed (70%+ accuracy, no hints, no retries). Counts as a Big-tier treat.
  • RP-... Report — session happened but didn't hit the small-code bar. Still tells you what was done; no treat earned by the rules but you can recognise effort.

The HMAC secret must match the one in index.html. Change both files if you want to rotate the secret (and clear Bertie's localStorage so he can't replay old codes).
